# Talking to Lex

Updated 2026-07-15. The old command list is gone. You do not memorize
phrases anymore; you just talk.

## How it works

You speak to Lex's voice directly, like a phone call. Small talk and
quick questions get answered on the spot. Anything that needs real
work goes to her deeper reasoning; she will tell you she is on it, and
the answer is spoken when it lands. While she is working you can keep
talking: asides get answered, new instructions join the work.

Controls are just words now. Say what you mean, any phrasing:

- "be quiet" / "shut up" - she stops talking
- "you can talk again" - speech comes back
- "hold on, stop what you're doing" - she aborts, recaps, and hands
  you the floor
- "stop listening" / "I'm back" - microphone off and on
- "turn voice off" - full voice teardown (restart it with the
  start-voice button)
- "we're done, end the session" - closes the session

If she is ever unsure whether you meant a control, she treats it as
conversation instead. Every control also has its dashboard button, and
the buttons always work.

## The one phrase to remember

**"lex emergency stop"** - immediate panic kill, no AI in the loop,
works even while she is mid-sentence. This is the only fixed phrase in
the whole system.
